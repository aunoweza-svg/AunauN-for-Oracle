## 💭 Honest Feedback

1. I chose a default synthetic voice and only learned after delivery that it sounded too artificial. I should have started with a short audition and let the user compare options before investing in the first full export.
2. The model catalogue reported OmniVoice installed despite an incomplete snapshot. Generic internal-error messaging did not expose the cause, and the retry also had a network error after some files had arrived. Checking the actual cache and performing a real synthesis exposed the distinction, but the watermark component remained unavailable.
3. I had no full audiovisual playback inspection in the host. A contact sheet, motion proxy, automatic transcript and loudness check help find problems, but cannot prove natural voice delivery, precise edits, absence of jitter, or rights to background music. The final handoff must ask the user to review and must not imply those checks were completed by ear.

## Lessons Learned

- **Pattern**: Build shot timing against the actual narration and verify the final file as both data and an audiovisual experience.
- **Mistake**: Do not equate installed-status with a complete cache or confuse the app name with the selected engine.
- **Discovery**: Copyright terms can differ across application, model weights, source voice and music; check the specific assets.
